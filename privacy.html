Privacy policy

This app is used only by its owner, to sync the owner's own Google Drive files with the owner's own server using rclone.

  • Files are copied only between the owner's Google Drive and the owner's server.
  • No data is collected, sold, or shared with third parties.
  • The access token is stored only on the owner's server.
  • Access can be revoked at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.
